# Code of Conduct

SplatCheck contributors and participants are expected to be respectful,
constructive, and precise. Focus criticism on code, formats, and reproducible
behavior—not on people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately
through GitHub's available repository reporting tools. If no private channel is
available, open a minimal issue without sensitive details to request one.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and a participant's
willingness to correct the behavior. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter
is prohibited.
